## Brand & Style

This design system blends timeless Ayurvedic wisdom with contemporary luxury commerce. Built for mindful consumers seeking holistic health, pure botanical formulations, and clinically backed traditional wellness, the design prioritizes calm clarity, scientific integrity, and deep organic warmth.

The visual style unites **Organic Minimalism** with **Editorial Heritage**:
- **Purity & Warmth:** Soft warm-tinted paper backgrounds replace sterile tech whites, establishing an earthy, reassuring baseline.
- **Botanical Prestige:** Deep forest green conveys dense phytotherapeutic potency and clinical herbal rigor, balanced by vibrant sprouting leaf greens and warm Ayurvedic gold accents reminiscent of turmeric and raw honey.
- **Restraint & Elevation:** Interfaces favor generous whitespace, deliberate typographical hierarchy, uncluttered product framing, and delicate low-contrast borders over noisy sales badges or aggressive promotional banners.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical botanical pharmacopeia:
- **Primary (`#1B4D3E`):** A disciplined, rich forest green serving as the cornerstone for primary brand elements, prominent headers, dark containers, and key calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#38A169`):** Fresh herbal green used to highlight vitality, benefits, trust metrics, verification badges, and positive active states.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059`):** Warm Ayurvedic gold that evokes handcrafted remedies, brass cauldrons, and sunlit botanicals. Reserved for premium badges, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Neutral (`#262626`):** Deep charcoal delivering optimal readability for running body copy without the harsh artificial glare of pure black.
- **Surfaces:** Dual-layer organic off-whites (`#FCF6EA` and `#F4F7F4`) provide warmth, while `#FFFFFF` remains dedicated to elevated card canvases and content panels.

## Typography

The type system balances ancestral authority with effortless digital legibility:
- **Display & Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Delivers editorial distinction, evoking classic herbal pharmacopoeias and scholarly Ayurvedic texts. Headings above 30px dynamically scale down for mobile viewports to prevent awkward line breaks on compact devices.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its humanist warmth, open apertures, and crisp rendering at micro sizes. Provides uncompromised readability for ingredient disclosures, dosage schedules, and clinical guarantees.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Kept uppercase or title case with subtle tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to lend a clean, apothecary-label feel to metadata, dosage tags, and ingredient highlights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm that honors generous negative space:
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with a centered maximum container width of `1280px`, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` page margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:**
  - Standard section spacing: `4rem` to `6rem` on desktop, compressed to `2.5rem` to `3.5rem` on mobile.
  - Card interior padding: `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` for product listings; `2rem` for featured trust anchors.
- **E-Commerce Alignment:** Product carousels transition into horizontal snap-scroll flows on mobile with half-card peeks (right-side overflow) to encourage continuous organic discovery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient shading combined with crisp, organic micro-borders:
- **Zero Elevation (Base Canvas):** Backing containers rely on `#FCF6EA` or `#F4F7F4` with no drop shadow.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Inputs):** Elevated by solid white fill (`#FFFFFF`), a soft perimeter stroke of `1px solid rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)`, and an ambient tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Subtle lift using `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav, Floating Cart & Modals):** Gentle botanical grounding using `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.12), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Botanical Accent Tier:** Trust cards and hero banners utilize deep rich fills (`#1B4D3E`) with layered translucent floral or sacred geometric watermark patterns at 5% opacity, creating tangible depth without excessive blur.

## Shapes

The interface embraces **Soft Roundedness** (`roundedness: 1`):
- **Base Geometry:** A baseline radius of `4px` (`0.25rem`) applied to input fields, trust badges, and standard cards provides disciplined architectural calm.
- **Interactive Elements:** Buttons, select chips, and modal sheets scale to `8px` (`0.5rem`) for a tactile, friendly touch.
- **Category Navigators & Avatar Seals:** Circular geometries (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for "Shop by Concern" circular icons, customer testimonial avatars, and traditional Ayurvedic seal stamps.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1B4D3E` with white text, font weight 600 (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), 8px border radius, 48px height (`px-6 py-3`). Hover transforms fill to `#0F5132` with a gentle 1px upward translateY.
- **Secondary / Cart Trigger:** Border `1.5px solid #1B4D3E`, background transparent, text `#1B4D3E`. Hover fills with `#1B4D3E` and switches text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Gold Accent Action:** Used for VIP packs and trial kits: `#C5A059` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, shifting to `#AA8743` on hover.

### 2. Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border (`#E5E1D8`), 8px radius. 
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratio 1:1 against a light natural tinted backdrop (`#F8F9F7`), showcasing bottled herbs and packaging with ample breathing room.
- **Tagging:** Subtle pill tags pinned top-left for "100% Ayurvedic" (`#F4F7F4` background with `#1B4D3E` text) or discount status (`#B9382B` badge).
- **Price Block:** Strikethrough MRP in `#686D65` accompanied by bold selling price in `#1B4D3E`. Star rating shown with `#C5A059` fill and count in parentheses.

### 3. Trust & Clinical Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Small uppercase text (`label-sm`), background `#FCF6EA`, border `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`, text `#AA8743`.
- **Feature Banners:** Full-width `#1B4D3E` container featuring circular fine-line icons showcasing "Triple-Tested for Safety," "Ayurveda Backed by Modern Research," and "100% Whole-Herb Extracts."

### 4. Chips & Concern Filters
- **Filter Tags:** Light warm-gray background (`#F4F7F4`), `rounded-full`, 36px height, subtle text (`#262626`).
- **Active Filter:** Filled with `#1B4D3E`, text `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by an organic leaf icon prefix.

### 5. Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** 48px height, 4px border radius, `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E5E1D8`. Focus ring uses `2px solid #38A169` with a delicate `0 0 0 3px rgba(56, 161, 105, 0.15)` glow.
- **Floating Labels:** Crisp, accessible charcoal microcopy (`label-md`).

### 6. Testimonial & Review Cards
- **Design:** `#FCF6EA` warm card with `1px solid #E8D5A3`, featuring 5 solid `#C5A059` stars, patient initials in an organic circular badge, and verified buyer botanical checkmarks.
